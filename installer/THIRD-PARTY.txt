This application bundles software produced by other projects. The components
below are redistributed under their own terms.


Qt 6 (The Qt Company and contributors)
--------------------------------------
Used under the GNU Lesser General Public License v3. Qt is linked dynamically
and its libraries ship as separate DLL files, so they can be replaced with
compatible builds. Source is available from https://download.qt.io/


FFmpeg (FFmpeg project)
-----------------------
ffmpeg.exe and ffprobe.exe are invoked as separate processes over their
documented command line interface. They are not linked into the application.

  >> The specific build shipped with this installer, and the licence that
  >> applies to it, are recorded in bin\FFMPEG-BUILD.txt.

FFmpeg is distributed in LGPL and GPL configurations depending on which
encoders and filters are compiled in. A GPL build carries obligations that an
LGPL build does not. This project targets an LGPL build providing NVENC
(h264_nvenc, hevc_nvenc) and libx264; that configuration excludes libx265,
libxvid, and several GPL-only filters.

Source is available from https://ffmpeg.org/
